Dental Swelling Can Require Emergency Medical Care

Some swelling symptoms may need emergency medical care instead of a dental referral call. Do not wait on a callback or dental appointment if symptoms appear life-threatening.

Call 911 or seek emergency medical care immediately if swelling spreads toward the throat, eye, or neck, or if symptoms include trouble breathing or swallowing, uncontrolled bleeding, major facial trauma, suspected broken jaw, loss of consciousness, severe head injury, or other life-threatening symptoms.
